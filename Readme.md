# RetainIQ

### E-Commerce Customer Intelligence & Retention Platform

**RetainIQ** is an end-to-end customer intelligence platform designed to help e-commerce businesses understand their customers, identify high-value segments, predict customer churn, and recommend actionable retention strategies.

Rather than being just a visualization dashboard, RetainIQ combines **data engineering, analytical SQL, customer segmentation, machine learning, and an interactive React dashboard** into a single system.

---

## 🚀 What Problem Does RetainIQ Solve?

E-commerce companies collect huge amounts of customer and transaction data, but raw transaction records don't directly answer important business questions.

RetainIQ is designed to answer:

* Who are our most valuable customers?
* Which customers are likely to churn?
* Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
* How well are we retaining customers?
* Which cohorts have the best long-term value?
* What products are frequently purchased together?
* How frequently do customers purchase?
* Which customers are becoming inactive?
* Which customers should the business prioritize for retention?
* What action should be taken for an at-risk customer?

The goal is to turn raw transaction data into **actionable customer intelligence**.

# 📊 Business Analytics

RetainIQ calculates several important e-commerce metrics.

### Customer Lifetime Value

Estimates the historical or predictive value generated by a customer.

### Average Order Value

```text
AOV = Total Revenue / Number of Orders
```

### Repeat Purchase Rate

```text
Repeat Purchase Rate =
Customers With 2+ Orders / Customers With 1+ Orders
```

### Retention Rate

Measures the percentage of customers who return and purchase again within the defined retention period.

### Purchase Frequency

Measures how frequently customers place orders.

### Revenue by Cohort

Tracks revenue generated by customers grouped according to their first purchase period.

### Customer Acquisition Cost

Where acquisition-cost data is available:

```text
CAC = Acquisition Cost / Number of Acquired Customers
```

### Product Affinity

Identifies products or categories that are frequently purchased together.

# 🧠 Model Comparison

RetainIQ evaluates multiple models rather than assuming one algorithm is best.

### Baseline

**Logistic Regression**

Provides an interpretable baseline.

### Ensemble

**Random Forest**

Captures nonlinear relationships between customer behavior and churn.

### Gradient Boosting

**XGBoost**

Used as a high-performance model for tabular customer data.

Models are evaluated using:

* Precision
* Recall
* F1-score
* ROC-AUC
* Confusion Matrix

Accuracy alone is not treated as the primary metric because churn datasets can be imbalanced.

---

# ⚠️ Churn Definition

Churn must be defined before training the model.

For example:

```text
A customer is considered churned if they have
not placed an order for N days after their expected
purchase window.
```

The actual churn window is determined based on the dataset and documented in the project.

The system avoids using future information when constructing training features to prevent **data leakage**.

# 🎯 Retention Recommendation Engine

RetainIQ goes beyond simply predicting churn.

Instead of displaying:

> Customer churn probability: 82%

the platform produces an actionable recommendation.

Example:

```text
Customer: C10291

Churn Probability: 82%
Risk Level: HIGH

Reason:
- 87 days since last purchase
- Historically high purchase frequency
- High lifetime value
- Strong discount response

Recommended Action:
Targeted 10% discount
```

Other possible recommendations include:

* Priority retention outreach
* Personalized product recommendation
* Re-engagement campaign
* Targeted discount
* Continue normal engagement

---

# ⚖️ Important ML Limitation

A standard churn model predicts **risk**, not the causal impact of a marketing intervention.

For example, if the model predicts:

```text
Churn probability = 82%
```

we should **not** automatically claim:

```text
10% discount → churn probability becomes 54%
```

unless historical intervention/treatment data exists to support such a claim.

Therefore, RetainIQ distinguishes between:

### Churn Prediction

> "This customer has an 82% probability of churn."

and:

### Recommended Action

> "This customer is high-risk and historically responds to discounts. Consider a targeted discount."

If suitable treatment/control data becomes available, the platform can be extended with **uplift modeling / causal inference**.

# ⚠️ Limitations

RetainIQ is a portfolio/research project and should not be treated as a production decision-making system without additional validation.

Important limitations include:

### Churn prediction is probabilistic

A predicted churn probability is not a guarantee that a customer will churn.

### Recommendations are not automatically causal

A recommendation does not prove that the suggested action will prevent churn.

### CLV depends on methodology

Different CLV methodologies can produce different estimates.

### Dataset quality affects results

Missing or biased transaction data can affect analytical and ML results.

### Synthetic data is not real customer behavior

If the demo dataset is synthetic, model performance should not be interpreted as real-world performance.
